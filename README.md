# AI in L&D Notes

Notes on how AI is changing learning and development: what works, what doesn't, and what's still unclear.

**These are my personal opinions.** I'm always open to a live discussion: write to me via the contacts below if anything here resonates with you.

## Focus areas

**Adaptive learning**  
Adjusting the path, not just the pace. Most systems adjust difficulty; few adjust direction.

**Voice simulation**  
Practicing real conversations in a safe environment. Works when the scenario feels real and failure is safe.

**Trigger-based learning**  
Short lessons at the moment of need. Better than scheduled modules, but harder to build.

**LLM-based agents**  
Guiding learners in context. The challenge is trust, cost, and knowing when not to use an agent.

**Vibe-coding for validation**  
Building prototypes without engineering. Great for testing ideas (not a substitute for a product).

## How I think about this

AI is not replacing instructors. It is making learning more personalized, timely, and measurable: if we design it around real behavior, not around the technology.

## Open questions

- How do we measure learning outcomes, not just engagement?
- When does personalization become noise?
- What does transformation actually look like in metrics?
